Presets

Click a key to change it.

  • Tap for a pass along the ground, hold for a harder or lofted ball.
  • Aftertouch: keep steering after the shot to bend the ball; forwards lifts it, backwards makes it dip.
  • The button without the ball is a slide tackle.
  • You automatically control whoever is nearest the ball.
  • Gamepads work too (controller 1 → blue, controller 2 → red).
  • Esc pauses.

One player opens a match and passes on the code; the other enters it. Whoever opens the match plays blue, the second player red. You control your own team with either set of keys from the table above.

Works between two browser tabs, between computers on the same network, and across the internet once you host server/relay.js somewhere.
